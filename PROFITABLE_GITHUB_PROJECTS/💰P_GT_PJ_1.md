*Key Points from Chapter One - Brief Descriptions*

1. *ICT Impact on Education*  
ICT has transformed education globally. Institutions now rely on digital tech for teaching, learning, research, and administration.

2. *Role of Computer Science Labs*  
Labs serve as centers for practical learning, training, workshops, innovation, and collaboration. They build technical skills for the digital society.

3. *Importance of Lab Management*  
Good management ensures smooth, efficient lab operations. Poor management leads to failure of lab activities. Core steps: planning, organizing, implementing, monitoring.

4. *Problems with Manual Systems*  
Most labs use manual methods for attendance, scheduling, inventory, maintenance. These are time-consuming, error-prone, and inefficient for large student populations.

5. *Consequences of Poor Management*  
Results in system breakdowns, software malfunction, virus infections, unauthorized access, poor maintenance, resource underutilization, scheduling conflicts, and high admin workload.

6. *Role of Automation Technologies*  
RFID, wireless monitoring, LIMS, and AI have improved lab administration and efficiency. RFID enables automated attendance, authentication, inventory, and access control.

7. *Introduction of Agentic AI*  
Agentic AI are intelligent systems capable of autonomous decision-making, adaptive learning, predictive analysis, and workflow optimization with minimal human input.

8. *Benefits of Agentic AI in Labs*  
Improves resource allocation, supports predictive maintenance, automates admin tasks, monitors equipment, and enables real-time data analysis.

9. *Existing Research Gap*  
Many tertiary labs lack intelligent automated systems. This causes frequent equipment failure, poor maintenance culture, inefficient scheduling, and safety risks like overheating and malware.

10. *Study Variables*  
*Dependent Variable*: Efficiency and productivity of lab operations.  
*Independent Variable*: Agentic AI-Driven Computer Science Academic and Laboratory Development System.

11. *Aim of the Study*  
Design and develop an Agentic AI-driven system to automate seat booking, attendance, scheduling, monitoring, and provide intelligent admin assistance.

12. *Specific Objectives*  
- Build role-based system for students, lecturers, admins  
- Implement AI-driven seat booking with limits  
- Create scheduling/reservation module for lecturers  
- Develop attendance system with activation codes  
- Build real-time monitoring dashboard with visual indicators

13. *Methodology: Case Study & RAD*  
Case study of University of Ibadan Computer Science New Lab. Uses Rapid Application Development model: Requirements Planning, User Design, Rapid Construction, Testing & Implementation.

14. *Data Collection Methods*  
Observation, interviews with admins/lecturers/students, questionnaires, and document review of lab records and schedules.

15. *Data Analysis Approach*  
Uses quantitative and qualitative methods. Thematic analysis identifies problems and opportunities for AI automation in lab management.

16. *System Design Components*  
Includes input/output design, database design, UI design, flowcharts, UML diagrams, system architecture, and AI agent interaction design with iterative prototyping.

17. *Implementation Tech Stack*  
HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, JavaScript for frontend. PHP for backend. MySQL for database. Includes Agentic AI interface for prompt-based admin tasks.

18. *Testing Strategy*  
Unit, integration, system, and user acceptance testing to verify functionality, performance, reliability, and usability. Focus on booking limits, access control, notifications, reports.

19. *Significance of the Study*  
Benefits students through easier access and learning support. Helps managers reduce paperwork and improve resource use. Aids institution in digital transformation and security.

20. *Scope and Limitations*  
Scope limited to UI Computer Science New Lab. Covers registration, scheduling, equipment mgmt, attendance, reporting. Limitations: 3-5 week timeframe, budget, limited data access, power/internet issues.
