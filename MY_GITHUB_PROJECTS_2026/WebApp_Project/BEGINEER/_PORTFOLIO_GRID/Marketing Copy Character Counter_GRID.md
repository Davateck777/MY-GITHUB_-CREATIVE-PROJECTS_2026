### MVP boundaries

- **Platform scope:** standard X posts, Google responsive search ad text assets, and one clearly identified Meta feed-ad preset—not every ad format or premium post length.
- **Scoring:** readiness feedback is not Google Ads Quality Score, platform approval, or a forecast of clicks and conversions.
- **Previews:** illustrative only; actual truncation and layout can vary by placement, device, and platform changes.
- **Privacy:** saved copy stays in the current browser unless exported. Local storage is not encrypted or automatically synced.
- **Excluded:** automatic publishing, account integration, live ad-policy review, collaboration, and AI-generated rewrites.
