### MVP boundaries
- **Privacy:** drafts stay in the current browser unless explicitly exported; local storage is not encrypted or automatically synced.
- **Scoring:** keyword density is descriptive, not a target to maximise. Recommendations favour natural language over keyword stuffing.
- **Preview:** mobile/desktop views are illustrative—not exact reproductions of LinkedIn’s changing interface.
- **Excluded:** automatic profile publishing, account integration, cloud collaboration, and AI-generated rewrites.
